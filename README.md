👋 Hi, I’m Matteo (he/him/his), and I am a digital and computational archaeologist at the University of Bern. I develop agent-based simulations and other computational solutions in archaeology, and coordinate the [CoDA Lab at the Institute of Archaeological Sciences](https://www.iaw.unibe.ch/research/bern_coda_lab/index_eng.html). My main research interests encompass cultural evolution, the simulation of ancient navigation, and data management and analysis - with a special interest for 3D data. I have published and worked on software in many different projects, among which my seafaring simulator, [Pytheas](https://github.com/mtomasini/PytheasSimulator), as well as my ABM implementation to study [metapopulation models of cultural evolution](https://github.com/mtomasini/MetapopulationsPython). 

Additionally to my research duties, I am a member of the board of [Nordic-RSE](https://nordic-rse.org/), an association dedicated to the advancement of careers of research software engineers in the Nordics, as well as the convener of the [special interest group for Scientific Scripting Languages in Archaeology](https://sslarch.github.io/) (part of CAA International).


- 👀 I’m especially interested in developing digital and computational tools to understand archaeology. To me, the archaeological question comes first, and the tool to be used is accessory to the ideas - not the other way around.
- 🌱 I’m currently improving my understanding of 3D technologies for applications behind documentation purposes.
- 💞️ I’m looking to create new collaborations in archaeology! If you think that computational approaches might help you, come to me!
